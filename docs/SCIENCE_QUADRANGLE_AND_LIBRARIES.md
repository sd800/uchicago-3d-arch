# Crerar, Science Quadrangle and Harper — 18 October 2026

The scope and source-to-model decisions are recorded in `sources/science_quadrangle_review.json`. Crerar is a detailed exterior reconstruction; Harper and four surrounding science buildings are optimized exteriors. These two standards remain distinct.

Crerar is rebuilt around its actual architectural organization: three above-ground floors and a lower level, a genuinely inset lower envelope below the documented 11-foot upper cantilever, thick limestone upper panels with cut-through apertures and recessed renovated glazing, an independently supported stone entrance arch, a double-height glazed arrival, granite steps/landing/handrails, and separate shaped large and small rooflights. The 2018 architect's floor plans and two exterior photographs are compared with the university elevation and library architectural facts. Generic classroom windows and an undifferentiated long glass roof box are removed. Published replacement-panel dimensions inform the upper bays. Local levels, the portal's placement within its mapped frontage, joints and small rooflight members remain exterior interpretations; the model does not claim measured construction accuracy or reconstructed interiors.

Harper retains the published 41.148 m tower peak and the existing presentation camera. Its high central reading-room range, eight principal Gothic window bays, two lower window levels, stone courses, connected pitched roof, corner turrets and upper tower lights are distinguished from a generic castle-like extrusion. Sculpture is abstracted. This is an optimized exterior, not a detailed conservation survey.

Kersten receives distinct street/quad facades and a glazed pitched atrium spine. Searle's incorrect Gothic roof is replaced with its modern flat laboratory envelope, four above-ground floors and four documented roof heat-recovery chambers. Hinds' service shafts and red-brick exhaust heads rise distinctly above its main roof; the unpublished section heights and interpreted facade rows remain estimates. Jones returns to a historic stone, slate-roof and Gothic-portal character. The adjacent medical buildings, Cummings and Gordon retain their preceding work.

Ground work is confined to the short connection between Crerar's stair apron and existing mapped paths, path-edge strips and entry tree clearance. Original Quad lawn/paths remain authoritative. Source images are reviewed remotely and are not redistributed as textures.

Validation compares all 171 unaffected building layers against the preceding release, checks the two authorized Highlight revisions separately from the remaining 17 protected Highlights, tests physical cantilever recession and the open entrance arch, preserves the other camera presets, reads back CAD/GLB/FBX, and reviews the actual exported meshes in the browser. Technical checks are not substituted for source-photo comparison.
