# Current satellite campus review

The existing UChicago model was compared with the current accessible Google Maps satellite mosaic on 2026-10-18. The interface credits Airbus, Maxar Technologies and Vexcel Imaging US and displays copyright 2026; it does not expose a verified acquisition date. The access date must not be presented as an imagery date.

Five overlapping sweeps cover northern housing/athletics/libraries, the Main and Science Quadrangles, the western medical district, Midway/south campus and the eastern school/institution corridor. Enlarged views resolve suspected Campus North, Hutchinson and Ida Noyes roof discrepancies. The 177 building layers are indexed by review district in `sources/satellite_campus_review.json`.

Confirmed corrections are Campus North's pale residential roof membranes versus the distinct olive low dining roof; Max Palevsky's blue-violet roof color; Henry Crown's brown-red pitched roof; verification of the retained red Ida Noyes wing roofs; and repeated Hull Court cross-gables. The simultaneous Main Quad reconstruction retains mapped lawn and path relationships and does not fill courtyard voids.

This is a district-level roof/plan comparison, not certification of all facade details or every small roof object. Tree cover, image shadows, perspective displacement and resolution limit minor-edge interpretation. Satellite imagery is combined with owner/architect and reliable street photographs for exteriors; undocumented member dimensions remain estimates. Source images are not redistributed.

Review coverage, access dates and imagery-date caveats are internal project records. They are not additional labels or notices in the public viewer interface.
