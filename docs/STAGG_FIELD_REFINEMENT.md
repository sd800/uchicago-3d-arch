# Stagg Field complex: restrained outdoor refinement

## Scope

Optimize the outdoor fields immediately west of Ratner Athletics. Preserve the 177 campus building models, their layers and camera presets. This is an optimized campus-scale exterior treatment, not a measured sports-facility survey or a reconstruction of spectator facilities.

## References reviewed

- [University athletics: Outdoor Athletic Complex](https://athletics.uchicago.edu/sports/2023/6/12/facilities-athletic-fields.aspx): north/south football orientation; 400 m, eight-lane Ted Haydon Track; eight tennis courts and two practice halves; baseball home faces southeast and softball home faces northwest; soccer shares the eastern grass beside Ratner.
- [University overhead photograph](https://athletics.uchicago.edu/images/2023/7/9/Ratner-fields-overhead.jpg): red oval track, dark striped football turf, pale field lines and goalposts, clay baseball infield, east grass and Ratner relationship. Reviewed in the browser at its native 1500 × 900 image size. The 2025 thumbnail in the facilities-hours page is a crop of this same view, rather than independent evidence of a new layout.
- [University 2024 construction notice](https://athletics.uchicago.edu/sports/2024/4/3/stagg-field-construction.aspx): removal of the west spectator stands. Do not recreate old grandstand seating from the earlier aerial.
- Saved OpenStreetMap snapshot: track relation 2425728; football pitch 182018490; baseball 182019200; southeast ball pitch 1374170524; eight mapped full tennis rectangles and two mapped practice halves. Source tags label both ball pitches baseball; the university's description identifies the southeast pitch as softball.

## Changes

- Smooth continuous lane geometry with eight 1.22 m lanes and a connected western sprint extension. The lane-one measurement line, 0.30 m from the inner edge, is approximately 400 m. The oval is fitted to the mapped footprint; local curve geometry is estimated.
- Proper football length and width, alternating turf strips, yard lines, restrained hash marks and connected north/south goalposts.
- Eight full tennis layouts with singles alleys, service boxes, center service lines, lightweight nets and simplified perimeter fencing. Two practice halves remain half length and do not acquire false central nets.
- Opposed baseball and softball clay infields, recognizable base diamonds, mound/batter areas, foul lines and simplified backstops.
- Approximate eastern grass soccer markings and lightweight goals, plus short access paving and team-side benches. The grass is shared with ball outfields; it is not replaced by a separate invented hard surface.
- Exclude the active pitches, courts, track and runoffs from both mapped and illustrative tree placement; retain planting beyond their boundaries.
- Distinct turf, court apron and clay materials participate in the existing stable cartographic surface order. Paint sits above its field surfaces in CAD too. Nets and fences use sparse rails and posts rather than dense wire meshes.

## Accuracy and limits

Regulation field/court and base-spacing dimensions inform the simplified geometry. Soccer pitch size/location, small fittings, fence heights and detailed curve fit are photo interpretations. The saved map is a dated snapshot. No old grandstand is added, and no claim is made about construction completed after the available images. Small furniture is intentionally simplified to bound browser cost.
