# UChicago highlight refinement

All 19 architectural highlights receive a dedicated exterior-detail pass. Existing bespoke building volumes, mapped courtyards and roof forms are retained except the explicitly rebuilt forms below. This remains a public-source exterior interpretation; opening positions and member dimensions are not a measured facade survey.

Shaped reveals follow the existing glass outlines, including pointed Gothic heads. Frames and transoms remain within each opening zone. Coping follows actual roof triangles, including concave plans, and stays supported on its roof finish. No automatic rooftop equipment boxes or arbitrary full-height facade rods are introduced. Repeated components retain separate material/feature ownership.

Hutchinson Commons is rebuilt as a single great-hall storey with tall pointed openings, exterior buttresses and a steep slate roof, replacing generic repeated classroom floors. Larger Gothic openings are retained within their modeled storey zones. Modern highlights receive close-range framed glazing and roof coping while keeping their existing distinctive massing.
