# Medical-side exterior optimization — 18 October 2026

This pass covers every one of the 40 ordinary building layers in the Medical filter. The existing Gordon Center Highlight is retained unchanged. The aim is recognizable, source-informed exterior form at campus-map scale, between a generic footprint extrusion and a detailed architectural reconstruction.

## Principal changes

- Center for Care and Discovery: ten occupied floors, continuous horizontal glazing, a geometrically recessed seventh-floor Sky Lobby, integrated dark plant plinth and two narrow raised heads. The published 245-foot maximum is retained; the intermediate sections are proportional estimates.
- Duchossois Center for Advanced Medicine: heavy lower clinical base, tall grouped upper curtain bays, pale precast corner piers and a supported Maryland Avenue entrance canopy.
- Comer: green-tinted glazing, pale vertical service blades, a two-storey entrance and seven occupied floors replace the generic brick slab. Comer East has a separate lower clinical range and upper patient-room bands.
- Knapp Center for Biomedical Discovery: two-storey stone base, eight upper glazed laboratory floors, green stair accent and setback dark roof plant.
- Donnelley/Knapp Research: distinct teaching and research volumes with glazed entrance bays. The mapped greenhouse is placed above a solid four-storey base instead of being rendered as a free-standing ground-level glass shed.
- Billings, Chicago Lying-In and Abbott: separate historic stone ranges, differentiated tower silhouettes, grouped openings and connected roofs. Original mapped courtyards remain open.
- Secondary clinical/research blocks: individual mapped footprints and courts, clinical bases, upper window bands, local entry frontages and bounded roof profiles. Parking garages use open slabs, columns and safety edges rather than office-style windows.

## Evidence and limits

The references below establish names, mapped organization and exterior character. Major hospitals and several historic buildings were compared with owner/architect photographs. The remaining smaller blocks use the public mapped footprint, owner inventory and a conservative exterior interpretation; they are not individually claimed as photograph-verified reconstructions. Heights without a published measurement, setbacks, opening spacing, canopy dimensions and small members remain estimates. The owner wayfinding PDF is a historical campus-layout reference, not evidence of current construction completion. No future/unmapped cancer pavilion has been added.

References are recorded per building in `sources/medical_exterior_review.json` and `sources/building_optimization.json`.

The exported medical geometry is checked for source-profile coverage, grounded entrances, greenhouse support, the recessed CCD Sky Lobby, differentiated historic towers and open mapped courts. Browser review includes all 40 layers and the assembled medical campus.
